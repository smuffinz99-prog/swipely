Frequently asked questions

What is Swipely?

A free, browser-based tool for making swipeable carousels and quote graphics for LinkedIn and Instagram. Pick a theme, type your text, download ready-to-post slides.

Is Swipely free?

Yes — make and download carousels free, no signup. Pro ($8/mo) removes the watermark and unlocks premium themes and square export.

What size are the carousels?

1080×1350 (4:5 portrait) and 1080×1080 (1:1 square) — the sizes that fill the feed on LinkedIn and Instagram.

Do I need design skills or an account?

Neither. There's no signup and no design skills required — type your text, pick a theme, and Swipely handles the layout.
